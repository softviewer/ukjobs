Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal data

Last updated: June 2026

🔒 UKJobs.live is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains exactly how we handle your personal data in plain English.

1. Who We Are

UKJobs.live ("we", "us", "our") is a UK-based job search platform operated as a sole trader business. We connect job seekers with employers across the United Kingdom.

For privacy questions, contact us at: info@ukjobs.live

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following personal data when you use our platform:

  • Account information: Name, email address, password when you register
  • CV and profile data: Employment history, education, skills, certifications and contact details included in uploaded CVs
  • Application data: Cover letters, phone numbers and any information submitted when applying for jobs
  • Employer information: Company name, business address, contact details and job posting details
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type and pages visited when you use our site

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • To connect job seekers with relevant employers and job opportunities
  • To allow employers to review applications and CVs submitted to their roles
  • To send you job alerts, application updates and service notifications
  • To maintain, secure and improve our website
  • To respond to your customer support requests

We will never sell your personal data to third parties or use it for purposes beyond those listed above.

4. How We Share Your Data

Sharing data between job seekers and employers is core to our service:

  • With employers: When you apply for a job, your CV, cover letter and contact details are shared with that employer only
  • With legal authorities: We may disclose data if required by UK law or to protect the safety of our platform
  • No third-party advertising: We do not share your data with advertisers or marketing companies

5. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary:

  • Active accounts — data kept while your account is open
  • Job applications — kept for 12 months after application date
  • Deleted accounts — all data permanently removed within 30 days

You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing info@ukjobs.live

6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Because we operate in the UK, you have the following rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct any inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure: Ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to object: Object to how we process your data
  • Right to portability: Request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@ukjobs.live and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies necessary for the site to function. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings at any time.

8. Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • Our site uses HTTPS encryption (the padlock in your browser)
  • We use Cloudflare to protect against attacks and unauthorised access
  • We never store passwords in plain text
  • Access to personal data is strictly limited to authorised personnel only

9. Third Party Services

We use the following trusted third-party services which may process your data:

  • Cloudflare — website security and performance (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting (GitHub Privacy Policy)
  • ImprovMX — email forwarding (ImprovMX Privacy Policy)

10. Children's Privacy

UKJobs.live is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at info@ukjobs.live and we will delete it immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on our website. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

12. Contact Us & Complaints

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • Email: info@ukjobs.live
  • Website: ukjobs.live

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or call 0303 123 1113.
