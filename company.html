About Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind is a pioneering AI safety and research company, headquartered in London's King's Cross. We're working to develop AI responsibly for the long-term benefit of humanity — from healthcare breakthroughs to climate solutions. Our team of world-class researchers and engineers work on some of the most challenging problems in science and technology.

Our mission is to solve intelligence, then use that to solve everything else. We believe AI has the potential to accelerate scientific discovery, unlock solutions to humanity's greatest challenges, and drive economic growth.
